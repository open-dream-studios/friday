Plumbing rough-in for any job that includes a master bath MUST be modeled
as ≥ 4 working days with a crew of 2.

**Rationale.** Will's nominal from transcript line 1942: "four days for
two guys." Three days only works for a single half-bath. Anything
involving multi-fixture master bath, W/D relocation, or extended vent
runs hits the 4d × 2 floor.

**Counter-example.** A standalone powder-room remodel with only a
toilet, sink, and short vent run can land at 3 days × 2 crew without
violating this rule's intent. When in doubt, add a job-level rule
override rather than mutating this company rule.
