# Plans extraction — Simons Addition (3725 SIMONS 012226.pdf)

Drawing set: Greyscale Design, LLC (Rebecca Lineberry, NCIDQ). Dated
01/22/2026. 5 sheets total: cover (p.1), left/back elevations (p.2),
right elevation (p.3), floor plan (p.4), roof plan (p.5).

## Structural decisions

- **Addition footprint locked at ~30' × 10'.** Floor plan (p.4) lists
  "1ST FLOOR ADDITION 295 sqft" and "BASEMENT ADDITION 295 sqft" for a
  "TOTAL ADDITION AREA 590 sqft." Matches scope's "approximately 30' x
  10'" objective.
- **Two-story vertical structure confirmed.** Elevations (p.2, p.3)
  show 8'-0" floor line to ceiling on both basement and first-floor
  addition levels, with a 1'-0" floor system band between them =
  ~17'-0" total wall height from grade to top plate.
- **Roof geometry: 3/12 new gable tying into existing 6/12.** Roof
  plan (p.5) annotates slopes "12 / 3" on the new addition and "12 /
  6" on the existing roof, matching scope. Multiple valleys (V) and
  ridges (R) are called out where the new gable meets the existing
  roof. Slope transitions (T) marked at the tie-in seam. (plans p.5)
- **Roof plan shows an existing chimney** that the new roof must be
  worked around (p.5). Not called out in scope's roof section — a
  cricket / flashing detail at the chimney will be needed.
- **Window schedule (p.4):** two NEW openings only —
  - `01` — 3'-0" × 1'-0" transom (qty 1)
  - `02` — 3'-0" × 3'-0" double-hung (qty 1)
  Plus TWO relocated existing windows shown on p.4 and p.2 (back and
  left elevations both call out "EXISTING WINDOW, RELOCATED").
- **Door schedule (p.4):** four doors —
  - `A` — 32" × 1-3/8" panel door (qty 2)
  - `B` — 30" × 1-3/8" panel door (qty 1)
  - `C` — 28" × 1-3/8" panel door (qty 1)
  - `D` — 36" × 1-3/4" exterior metal door (qty 1)
- **Elevator shaft dimensioned 4'-0" × 4'-0"** on both floors of the
  addition (p.4), stacked vertically. Two "ELEV." tags shown — one
  first-floor level, one basement level.
- **Exterior finish material choices are explicit on elevations
  (p.2, p.3):** "NEW LAP SIDING" above and "NEW BRICK TO MATCH
  EXISTING" at the base of the addition on all three visible
  elevations. Roof calls out "NEW SHINGLES TO MATCH EXISTING" (p.2).

## Retrofit indicators (existing conditions the plans expose)

- **Two existing windows are being relocated, not just added.** Left
  and back elevations (p.2) show "EXISTING WINDOW, RELOCATED" callouts.
  Floor plan (p.4) also carries "RELOCATED EXISTING WINDOW" and
  "existing window, relocated" notes on the primary bedroom side.
  Involves cutting a new opening in the existing wall AND infilling
  the original opening. Neither the wall-patch labor nor a separate
  "window infill" line is broken out in the scope.
- **Existing door being relocated** — floor plan (p.4) labels
  "RELOCATED DOOR" at the seam between existing structure and new
  addition. Suggests reworking of an existing wall opening.
- **New addition ties into existing primary bedroom.** Floor plan
  (p.4) shows the existing primary bedroom wall being opened to
  connect to the new addition (this is where the 15'-6" LVL spans,
  per scope). The plans confirm the LVL location is at the interior
  bedroom / new-addition seam, not an exterior wall.
- **Existing hall bath is on the plan** (labeled "BTH. 2" on p.4)
  adjacent to the primary bath and closets. The plan confirms the
  hall bath is part of the addition-facing wall system, meaning any
  retrofit work there is physically entangled with the new-wall
  framing on the shared partition.
- **"CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY LOCATION OF EXISTING PLUMBING, VENTING,
  SEPTIC TANK, AND HVAC BEFORE CONSTRUCTION"** — hard note on floor
  plan (p.4). Explicit field-verification requirement.
- **"CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY EXISTING ROOF SLOPES AND SOFFIT DEPTH
  BEFORE CONSTRUCTION"** — hard note on roof plan (p.5). The 6/12
  existing pitch assumption is documented as needing field
  confirmation.
- **Standard boilerplate on every sheet:** "CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY ALL
  DIMENSIONS BEFORE CONSTRUCTION. GREYSCALE DESIGN, LLC WILL NOT BE
  RESPONSIBLE FOR ERRORS IN DIMENSIONS OR CALCULATIONS ON DRAWINGS."
  Puts full field-verify risk on the builder.

## Things plans show scope doesn't mention

- **Chimney in the roof-tie zone** (plans p.5). Scope's roof section
  does not mention chimney flashing, cricket, or working around an
  existing chimney. This is likely a scope omission — cricket
  flashing on a 3/12 gable meeting a masonry chimney is real labor
  and material.
- **Two window relocations vs one new opening cost.** Scope says
  "Install (4) windows including (3) 36" × 60" and (1) transom" but
  the plan window schedule (p.4) only lists TWO new windows (one
  3'-0" × 3'-0" DH and one 3'-0" × 1'-0" transom) plus TWO relocated
  existing windows. This is a real discrepancy in fenestration count
  — see Discrepancies below.
- **Room labels on the plan** that scope does not explicitly enumerate:
  primary bedroom is labeled with "HIS" and "HERS" closet zones (p.4)
  and a "LINEN" closet in both the primary bath area AND near the
  hall bath. Scope mentions a single walk-in closet; plans show a
  split closet system. This is likely what feeds the $7,000 optional
  "Closet & Cabinet System" package — plans are showing the design
  intent for that package.
- **"BENCH" and "RTRN." callouts inside primary bath** (p.4). The
  bench is in scope ("shower niche and bench") but the return
  ("RTRN.") — likely a return wall at the shower entry — is a tile
  detail not spelled out in scope prose.
- **Front porch and covered deck are drawn on the floor plan** (p.4)
  as existing conditions on the front and rear of the house. Scope
  never mentions these — they are context for the addition's tie-in
  points but not scope items.
- **Interior layout of the new second floor is fully designed**
  (p.4): primary bedroom, primary bath (with dual vanity, toilet,
  shower with bench, HIS/HERS closets), hall bath ("BTH. 2"), and a
  linen closet. Scope enumerates the fixtures but doesn't communicate
  the room-adjacency picture. Plans confirm layout matches scope's
  fixture list.
- **Basement addition floor plan shows storage / garage / lounge /
  living / kitchen-dining zones** (p.4). Scope only calls the
  basement level "storage space" and "basement storage finish."
  Plans depict a much more finished basement layout (garage, lounge,
  living, kitchen/dining) — but these appear to be labels of the
  EXISTING basement being connected to, not the new 295-sqft
  addition. Should be verified with PM.
- **Dryer vent relocation is called out on floor plan** (p.4:
  "RELOCATE DRYER VENT"). Scope covers W/D relocation but the
  physical vent-run through the roof is drawn on the plans as a
  specific detail.
- **New shower dimensioned on plan** (p.4) at ~5'-7½" × 10'-0"
  approximately in the primary bath. Scope calls the shower "approx.
  7' × 4'" — this doesn't match the plan proportions. See
  Discrepancies.

## Discrepancies (explicit scope-vs-drawings contradictions)

- **Window count mismatch.** Scope: "Install (4) windows including
  (3) 36" × 60" and (1) transom window." Plans (p.4 window schedule):
  only TWO new windows — one 3'-0" × 3'-0" DH and one 3'-0" × 1'-0"
  transom. The remaining two windows shown on elevations are
  RELOCATED existing units, not new. The scope's material allowance
  ($300/window × 4 = $1,200) may overstate new-window material cost
  by half, OR the plans undercount and two more new units are
  expected. PM must reconcile before ordering.
- **Window size mismatch.** Scope names "36" × 60"" (3'-0" × 5'-0")
  as the three new units. Plans schedule lists the single new DH as
  "3'0" × 3'0"" (3'-0" × 3'-0"), not 3'-0" × 5'-0". Different
  supplier order.
- **Master shower dimensions differ.** Scope: "custom tile shower
  approx. 7' × 4'." Plans (p.4) show a shower footprint in the
  primary bath that reads closer to 5'-7½" × 10'-0" region (or a
  shower area within that zone) — need PM/designer walkthrough to
  resolve. Tile-quantity implications ($3/sqft walls, $6/sqft mosaic
  floor allowances) shift with the true dimensions.
- **Exterior siding material mismatch.** Scope calls for "vinyl
  siding to match existing as closely as possible." Plans elevations
  (p.2, p.3) call out "NEW LAP SIDING" (no material specified in the
  note) AND "NEW BRICK TO MATCH EXISTING" at the base band on all
  three visible elevations. Plans show a MIXED brick-base +
  lap-siding-above facade. Scope only mentions siding. Brick base is
  a real cost item (masonry crew, brick match, mortar) not carried
  in the breakdown. Significant potential change order.
- **Door count.** Scope: "Provide and install (4) interior doors
  total. Assume (1) pre-hung interior doors and (2) pocket door
  systems." Plans (p.4) door schedule totals 5 doors: 2× 32" (A), 1×
  30" (B), 1× 28" (C), and 1× 36" exterior metal (D). Interior door
  count from the plan is 4 (A×2 + B + C); scope's "(4) interior
  doors total" matches the interior count, but scope also says "(1)
  pre-hung interior + (2) pocket doors" which totals only 3. Plan
  gives no pocket-door callout that's visible in extracted text.
  Reconcile with designer.
- **Roof tie-in note vs scope assumption.** Scope: "Roof tie-in
  assumes standard integration; additional structural modifications
  not included." Plans (p.5) explicitly demand contractor verify
  existing slopes and soffit depth. Field discovery at the tie-in is
  the most likely change-order trigger — plans put the risk in
  writing, scope disclaims it.
- **Septic / grinder pump.** Scope (Pre-Construction section)
  describes TDEC septic inspection, potential relocation, and a
  grinder-pump option — all as change-order candidates. Plans (p.4)
  say only "CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY LOCATION OF EXISTING PLUMBING,
  VENTING, SEPTIC TANK, AND HVAC BEFORE CONSTRUCTION." No septic
  detail is drawn. The plans do not contradict scope, but they
  confirm that no septic solution has been designed — everything
  waits on TDEC.
