## Discrepancies (SCOPE vs DRAWINGS)
- **Siding material.** Scope says "Install vinyl siding to match existing as closely as possible" `(scope.md L82)`. Elevation drawings ALL THREE (left / back / right) explicitly label "**NEW BRICK TO MATCH EXISTING**" AND "**NEW LAP SIDING**" `(pdf L44-56; L62-73)`. Drawings imply a dual-material exterior (brick base + lap siding above); scope specifies vinyl siding only. **Material and cost implications: brick veneer is materially different from vinyl lap siding and requires masonry crew + longer install.** MUST be resolved before framing / exterior finish phase. See questions.md q.siding_scope_vs_drawings.
- **Window schedule mismatch.** Scope says "(3) 36" x 60" DH + (1) transom" — implies 3 NEW DH windows. Plans' window schedule only lists 1 new transom + 1 new 3'0" x 3'0" DH (from the schedule table). Other DH windows in plans are labeled "RELOCATED EXISTING." This is not a total conflict (scope may be quoting 3 windows in the addition regardless of new vs relocated) but the DH quantities disagree with the schedule table. Confirm final new-window count for procurement.
- **Roof tie-in complexity.** Roof plan page 5 shows multiple valleys at the tie-in — this is not a simple gable-to-gable connection but a valleyed junction against an existing hip / gable roof AND a chimney. Elevated Rule 4-addition concealed-roof-tie-in risk.
