# Drawing-set distillation — 308 Evergreen Street

Source: `3725 SIMONS 012226.pdf` — 5 pages from Greyscale Design, LLC (Rebecca Lineberry, NCIDQ). Sheets:

1. Cover sheet (project title)
2. Left + Back elevations (1/4" = 1'-0")
3. Right elevation (1/4" = 1'-0")
4. Floor plan (1/4" = 1'-0")
5. Roof plan (1/4" = 1'-0")

Drawings are conceptual / interior-designer set, NOT structural-stamped. Sheet 1 notes "Contractor to verify all dimensions before construction. Greyscale Design, LLC will not be responsible for errors in dimensions or calculations on drawings."

## Structural decisions visible in plans

- **Roof pitch confirmed**: new portion = 3/12 SLOPE; existing = 6/12 (sheet 5). Tie-in geometry shown with ridge (R), slope (S), valley (V), transition (T) marks.
- **New ridge intersects existing roof** at a valley (V) on the front side — concealed tie-in zone identified.
- **Roof framing approach**: not specified on drawings — consistent with scope's "truss or rafter framed, determined during design phase."
- **New chimney noted** on roof plan ("CHIMNEY" label) — confirm whether this is the existing chimney passing through the new addition footprint or a new flue. Not mentioned in scope text.
- **Elevations show**: new lap siding, new brick to match existing, new shingles to match existing, 8'-0" floor-to-ceiling on both stories, 1'-0" floor-system depth between (sheet 2-3).
- **Window placements visible** on elevations: relocated existing windows on left + back elevations; new windows on right elevation. Sizes match scope's (3) 36"×60" DH + (1) 3'×1' transom.

## Floor plan readings (sheet 4)

- **Footprint**: "1st floor addition" 295 sf + "basement addition" 295 sf = 590 sf "Total addition area" (annotation on sheet 4).
- **Upper floor (addition zone)**: BDRM. 2 + BEDRM. 3 visible (existing); new PRIMARY BEDROOM (29'-5" × 10'-0") + PRMRY. BTH. (with HERS / HIS / LINEN / BENCH / SHWR.) + walk-in closet (visible).
- **Existing-area annotations on floor plan**: "EXISTING WINDOW, RELOCATED", "RELOCATED EXISTING WINDOW", "EXISTING DOOR", "RELOCATED DOOR".
- **Hall bath area visible** but not detailed for the modifications — annotation "BTH. 2" appears.
- **ELEV. 4'-0"** annotation appears in TWO spots (upper and lower floor) — the future elevator shaft. Plans show the shaft INSIDE the addition footprint, NOT in existing area. **This resolves Signal C ambiguity for the elevator shaft — it's NEW construction, not retrofit.**
- Lower floor (basement zone): STORAGE, GARAGE, LOUNGE, LIVING, KIT, DINING, COVERED DECK, FRONT PORCH visible — existing house layout shown for context. New basement addition is STORAGE.
