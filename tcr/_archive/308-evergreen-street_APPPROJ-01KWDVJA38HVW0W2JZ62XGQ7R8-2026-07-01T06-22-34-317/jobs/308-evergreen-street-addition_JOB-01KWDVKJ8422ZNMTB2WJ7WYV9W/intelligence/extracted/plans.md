## Discrepancies (explicit scope-vs-drawings contradictions)

1. **Total addition area — 590 sqft vs. 789 sqft.** Plans sheet 4 states "590 sqft TOTAL ADDITION AREA" (295 first floor + 295 basement). CSV template header says footprint is **789 sqft**. Delta = 199 sqft. Possible explanations: (a) CSV's 789 sqft also counts the second-floor primary bedroom remodel envelope (~200 sqft) as "footprint" for pricing purposes; (b) CSV was populated from an earlier plan revision; (c) plans understate — check second floor. The scope narrative says "second-floor expansion of the primary bedroom" implying the addition extends UP over an existing footprint, which would make the finished "addition area" larger than the ground-plan 30×10 = 300 sqft × 2 = 600 sqft rough. **Confirm with PM.**

2. **Window count — 4 in scope, 2 in plans schedule.** Scope: "(3) 36" × 60" DH + (1) transom" = 4 windows. Plans WINDOW SCHEDULE (sheet 4): (1) 3'0"×1'0" transom + (1) 3'0"×3'0" DH = only 2 windows. But elevations show (2) "relocated existing window" labels + (1) "new window" + (1) "new transom window" = 2 new windows + 2 relocated existing. Reconciling: scope's "(4) windows" likely counts (2) new + (2) relocated. Only the (2) NEW ones are on the procurement schedule; the (2) relocated are re-installations of existing units. **Order only (2) windows: (1) 3'×3' DH + (1) 3'×1' transom.** Plus scope's (3) 36"×60" DH does NOT match plans' (1) 3'0"×3'0" — dimensional mismatch. Confirm with PM: is scope's window schedule out-of-date, or are plans understating?

3. **Truss vs. rafter unresolved.** Roof plan sheet 5 shows slopes but does not specify truss vs. stick framing. Consistent with scope's "determined during design phase" but leaves the framing ambiguity open for PM/design confirmation. Per addition_rules, default is stick-frame for small additions of this size (600 sqft footprint, span < 24').

4. **Chimney interaction on roof tie-in.** Plans clearly show a chimney adjacent to the tie-in. Scope's "roof tie-in assumes standard integration; additional structural modifications not included" applies; the chimney flashing + framing-around detail may be a change-order candidate.

5. **Address / project name mismatch.** Drawings are for "SIMONS ADDITION" but job manifest says "308 Evergreen Street" and CSV project label is "Simons Addition – 30'×10' Two-Story + Bedroom Remodel". The project is almost certainly the Simons family at 308 Evergreen Street — the "647 AA Deakins Rd" on the drawings is Greyscale Design's studio address, not the site. Non-issue but noted for records.
