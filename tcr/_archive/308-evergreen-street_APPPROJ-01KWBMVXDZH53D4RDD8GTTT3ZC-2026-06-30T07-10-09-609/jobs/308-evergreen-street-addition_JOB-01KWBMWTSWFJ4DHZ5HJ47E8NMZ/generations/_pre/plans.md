# Plans Interpretation — Simons Addition (308 Evergreen Street)

**Source drawings:** 3725 SIMONS 012226.pdf (5 sheets, Greyscale Design LLC, January 22 2026)
**Sheets present:** Sheet 1 — Title / Cover; Sheet 2 — Left & Back Elevations; Sheet 3 — Right Elevation; Sheet 4 — Floor Plan; Sheet 5 — Roof Plan

---

## Structural decisions

- **LVL beam — load-bearing wall opening.** Floor plan (Sheet 4) shows the addition attaches to the existing structure along the full 29'–5" north wall. Scope confirms a 3-ply 14" LVL spanning approx. 15'–6" to open an existing load-bearing wall. The LVL load path runs between the existing first floor and the new addition's floor system. Temporary shoring is required before LVL install; shoring must remain in place until LVL is fully seated and bearing.

- **Two-story floor system over basement.** Sheet 4 labels two area calculations: 295 sqft "1ST FLOOR ADDITION" and 295 sqft "BASEMENT ADDITION" = 590 sqft total addition area. The floor system above the basement (separating basement storage from 1st-floor living level) must carry live load from the primary bedroom above, requiring dimensional or engineered members per span. The plans note "CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY LOCATION OF EXISTING PLUMBING, VENTING, SEPTIC TANK, AND HVAC BEFORE CONSTRUCTION" — structural framing cannot be fully designed without field verification.

- **Roof tie-in pitch transition.** Sheet 5 (Roof Plan) shows a new gable roof at 3/12 pitch tying into the existing 6/12 roof system. The roof plan labels ridge (R), slope (S), valley (V), and a transition point (T) at the junction. Multiple valleys are indicated. This pitch-change tie-in is inherently a concealed-conditions risk: if existing rafters are not where drawings assume, a change order may be required. Sheet 5 also calls out "CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY EXISTING ROOF SLOPES AND SOFFIT DEPTH BEFORE CONSTRUCTION."

- **Elevator shaft — framing only.** Sheet 4 shows two "ELEV." callouts at approximately 4'–0" × 4'–0" in plan. One is at the basement level (south portion), one at the 1st-floor addition level. The shaft spans both floors and must be framed per code for future elevator installation; no elevator equipment is in scope. The shaft location is within the new addition footprint (not a retrofit).

- **Exterior door at back elevation.** Sheet 2 (Back Elevation) shows "NEW DOOR" at the lower level (basement level, 6'–8" clear opening), consistent with the exterior metal door (Type D: 36" × 1-3/4") in the door schedule on Sheet 4.

- **Roof chimney.** Sheet 5 labels a "CHIMNEY" within or adjacent to the new roofing area. Scope does not mention the chimney. This is a potential concealed-conditions item — the chimney may require flashing integration with the new roof system and could affect the tie-in structural assumptions.

## Retrofit indicators

The addition objective areas are: **primary bedroom**, **master bathroom**, **walk-in closet** (all on 2nd/1st floor of addition), and **basement storage**. Features in the plans that sit outside these named areas are flagged below.

- **Hall Bathroom (BTH. 2) — Sheet 4.** The floor plan labels "BTH. 2" adjacent to the existing structure (clearly in the existing floor plan area, not within the addition footprint). The room contains "NEW SHWR." callout, confirming a new shower installation in the existing hall bath space. This is physically separate from the addition footprint. Retrofit signal: **Signal A** (area mismatch — "BTH. 2" is not in the addition objective list) + **Signal B** ("existing" hall bath, drawn in existing house body). The scope section "Existing Hall Bathroom Modification" confirms this is retrofit.

- **BDRM. 2 and BEDRM. 3 — Sheet 4.** The floor plan labels two bedrooms ("BDRM. 2" and "BEDRM. 3") in the existing house portion. These are labeled adjacent to the addition boundary line but appear to be existing rooms, not new construction. Scope references "Primary Bedroom Remodel (~12'5" × 16')" which likely corresponds to one of these rooms. Retrofit signal: **Signal A** (these bedroom labels are not in the addition objective areas of primary bedroom / master bath / closet / basement storage) + **Signal C** (spatial location is in the existing house body, not within the new footprint). Confirm with PM which labeled room is the "primary bedroom remodel" and whether BDRM. 2 / BEDRM. 3 have any scope work beyond what's listed.

- **Relocated existing windows — Sheet 2 & Sheet 4.** Sheet 2 (Left & Back Elevations) labels two "EXISTING WINDOW RELOCATED" callouts in the existing structure walls. Sheet 4 also labels "EXISTING WINDOW, RELOCATED." Window relocations in existing walls are retrofit work (existing wall surgery), not part of adding new construction. Retrofit signal: **Signal B** ("existing" keyword, relocation of existing elements).

- **Dryer vent relocation — Sheet 4.** Sheet 4 labels "RELOCATE DRYER VENT" in the existing house area. This is work on the existing structure, not within the new addition footprint. Retrofit signal: **Signal B** ("relocate" of existing element) + **Signal A** (not in addition objective areas).

- **Garage / Lounge / Living / Kit. Dining / Covered Deck / Front Porch — Sheet 4.** The floor plan shows existing rooms (Garage, Lounge, Living, Kit/Dining, Covered Deck, Front Porch) labeled in the existing house. No scope work is called out for these areas, but their presence on the plan confirms the addition attaches to an occupied existing home. No retrofit work is currently scoped for these areas; noting for awareness only.

- **Hallway switch relocation — Scope only, not clearly placed on drawings.** Scope mentions "Relocate (1) existing switch in hallway including drywall patch, priming, and painting." This is in an existing hallway area (not within addition objective areas). Not explicitly called out on plans by label. Retrofit signal: **Signal B + Signal C** (existing hallway, spatial location unconfirmed on drawings — presume retrofit).

## Things plans show that scope doesn't mention

- **Brick veneer at lower level (basement level) on two elevations.** Sheet 2 (Left & Back Elevations) and Sheet 3 (Right Elevation) show "NEW BRICK TO MATCH EXISTING" at the lower-level (basement) portion of the addition exterior. The scope says only "Install vinyl siding to match existing as closely as possible" — no mention of brick veneer. The drawings show a two-material exterior: brick at the lower/basement level, lap siding above. This is a significant scope/drawings discrepancy (see Discrepancies section). Masonry is not in the cost breakdown sections and no masonry crew is budgeted.

- **Chimney shown on roof plan.** Sheet 5 shows a "CHIMNEY" feature at the roof level. Scope does not mention chimney work, chimney flashing, or chimney integration. This may be an existing chimney that the new roof must flash around; it is not called out in the scope and has no associated cost line.

- **Two "ELEV." (elevator shaft) openings shown on floor plan.** Sheet 4 shows two distinct elevator shaft callouts — one at basement level, one at first-floor addition level. The scope mentions "Frame (1) future elevator shaft approximately 4' × 4'" and electrical rough-in only. The drawings confirm both floors require the shaft framing opening, which is consistent with scope language but worth noting explicitly: the framing cost covers a two-story shaft penetration.

- **Window schedule shows only 2 windows (not 4).** Sheet 4 Window Schedule lists: Window 01 = 3'0" × 1'0" transom (qty 1) and Window 02 = 3'0" × 3'0" D.H. (qty 1). That is 2 windows total per the schedule. The scope states "Install (4) windows including (3) 36" × 60" and (1) transom window." There is a significant discrepancy in both count and dimensions between the drawings schedule and the scope (see Discrepancies section).

- **Door schedule shows 5 total doors (4 interior + 1 exterior).** Sheet 4 Door Schedule: Type A = 32" panel door (qty 2), Type B = 30" panel door (qty 1), Type C = 28" panel door (qty 1), Type D = 36" × 1-3/4" exterior metal door (qty 1). Total = 5 doors. Scope says "(4) interior doors total" + "(1) new exterior door" = 5 doors — this is broadly consistent (4 interior + 1 exterior), but door sizes differ from scope's allowance list which only specifies interior door quantities, not sizes. The 32" panel doors (Type A, qty 2) are larger than typical bedroom doors; confirm with PM.

- **Return/bench in master bathroom area.** Sheet 4 shows "RTRN. BENCH" in the primary bathroom area. The scope mentions a shower bench ("Install shower niche and bench"), consistent with this callout. No discrepancy — confirming plans align with scope on the bench.

- **"His" and "Hers" closet areas.** Sheet 4 labels separate "HIS" and "HERS" areas within the primary bedroom/closet zone, with a "LINEN" closet. The scope refers to "walk-in closet" as a single space. The drawings show a more articulated closet layout with distinct his/hers zones. This has implications for the optional closet/cabinet system ($7,000 allowance) — the space is more complex than a single walk-in.

- **72" dimension labeled in primary bath area.** Sheet 4 shows "72"" in the primary bathroom zone, consistent with the "Install 72" double vanity" scope line. Confirming alignment.

- **Roof plan shows multiple valleys at tie-in.** Sheet 5 shows three valley (V) points at the connection between the new 3/12 gable and the existing 6/12 roof geometry. Valleys are the highest-risk flashing points. The scope says "Roof tie-in assumes standard integration; additional structural modifications not included" — the multi-valley complexity is consistent with the change-order caveat in scope but is more pronounced in the drawings than scope language implies.

## Discrepancies

1. **Exterior cladding — vinyl siding vs. brick veneer.** Scope (line 67) states "Install vinyl siding to match existing as closely as possible." Drawings (Sheets 2 & 3, Left/Back/Right Elevations) show "NEW BRICK TO MATCH EXISTING" at the lower level (basement level) AND "NEW LAP SIDING" at the upper level. The plans show a two-material exterior with brick at the foundation/basement level. Scope has no brick line item and the cost breakdown section "Exterior Finishes – Siding, Trim & Gutters" ($5,731 total) does not appear to include masonry. **This is a material and budget discrepancy.** PM must clarify: is brick veneer in scope at the lower level? If so, a masonry cost line is missing from the breakdown.

2. **Window count and dimensions mismatch.** Scope states 4 windows: (3) 36" × 60" D.H. + (1) transom. Drawings Window Schedule (Sheet 4) lists only 2 windows: one 3'0" × 1'0" transom and one 3'0" × 3'0" D.H. — neither matches the scope's 36" × 60" spec, and count is 2 vs. 4. Possible explanations: (a) the drawing schedule is an early draft and scope is more current; (b) some windows are in the existing structure (relocated, not new-to-schedule); (c) the schedule was never fully populated. PM must reconcile before windows are ordered — procurement lead time of 14–21 days means this must be resolved during pre-construction.

3. **Existing windows labeled "relocated" on drawings but not explicitly costed in scope.** Sheets 2 and 4 show two existing windows being relocated ("EXISTING WINDOW RELOCATED"). The scope section "Exterior Finishes" does not call out window relocation; it only calls out new window installation. Window relocation involves framing modification, flashing, finish work on both the old opening (infill) and new opening — this may be embedded in the "Primary Bedroom Remodel" framing allowance or may be an uncaptured cost. PM to verify.

4. **Dryer vent relocation — scope says "washer and dryer relocation" but plans show only dryer vent.** Sheet 4 labels "RELOCATE DRYER VENT" specifically. The plumbing section of scope includes "Install washer and dryer relocation including water, drain, and roof venting," which is broader than just the vent. The plan label is consistent but partial — the full W/D relocation scope (supply lines, drain, vent) should be confirmed as included in the cost.

5. **Septic/TDEC — scope flags as change-order but plans note "CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY LOCATION OF EXISTING PLUMBING, VENTING, SEPTIC TANK, AND HVAC BEFORE CONSTRUCTION" (Sheet 4).** Scope treats septic work as excluded (change order basis). The drawings explicitly call out the need to verify before construction. This is not a contradiction but a risk flag: if the septic system location conflicts with the new addition footprint or excavation path, a change order will be required before site work can proceed. PM should complete the TDEC septic inspection during pre-construction and not assume it is clear.

6. **Roof framing method ambiguous.** Scope says "truss or rafter framed, determined during design phase." Plans (Sheet 5, Roof Plan) show a detailed roof geometry with multiple ridges, valleys, and a pitch transition — this complexity strongly favors stick-framing (rafters) over trusses, since custom trusses for a multi-valley transition roof with a pitch change would require engineered shop drawings and a 28–42 day lead time. The drawings do not resolve the ambiguity explicitly; however, the roof geometry makes trusses unlikely without significant cost and lead time. Default to stick-frame per TCR's company belief for small additions; flag for PM confirmation.
