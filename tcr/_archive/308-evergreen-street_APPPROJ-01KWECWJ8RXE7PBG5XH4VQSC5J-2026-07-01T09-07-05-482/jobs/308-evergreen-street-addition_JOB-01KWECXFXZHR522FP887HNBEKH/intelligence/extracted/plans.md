# Extracted — Architectural Plans (Simons Addition, dated 01/22/26)

Drawings by Rebecca Lineberry, NCIDQ / Greyscale Design, LLC. 5-sheet
set: cover / left+back elevations / right elevation / floor plan / roof
plan. Scale 1/4" = 1'-0" throughout. Contractor-verify note appears on
every sheet ("CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY ALL DIMENSIONS BEFORE CONSTRUCTION")
plus a specific field-verify callout on the floor plan for existing
plumbing, venting, septic tank, and HVAC location, and on the roof plan
for existing roof slopes and soffit depth.

## Structural decisions

- **Two-story addition, 30' × 10' footprint on drawings** matches scope
  objective. Floor plan (plans p.4) shows first-floor and basement each
  labeled `295` sqft, `TOTAL ADDITION AREA 590` — this is the addition
  footprint sqft called out on-sheet. (The CSV/summary lists 789 sqft
  overall footprint; see Discrepancies.)
- **Wall heights: 8'-0" per floor** on both stories (plans p.2 L/back
  elevations, plans p.3 right elevation), with a **1'-0" floor-system
  band** shown between the two floor lines. Door / window head heights
  called out at **6'-8"** and one **7'-0"** window head.
- **Roof: new gable, 3/12 slope tying into existing 6/12** — confirmed
  on roof plan (plans p.5, labels "12 S3" for new slope and "12 6" for
  existing). Multiple valleys (V), ridges (R), and slope-transitions
  (T) shown where new roof meets existing. Chimney noted on existing
  side of tie-in.
- **New shingles to match existing** and **new brick to match existing**
  called out on multiple elevations (plans p.2, p.3).
- **Exterior cladding is mixed: NEW LAP SIDING above + NEW BRICK TO
  MATCH EXISTING at base** on left, back, and right elevations
  (plans p.2, p.3). This is more nuanced than scope's "vinyl siding"
  language (see Discrepancies).
- **Second-floor (primary suite) program per floor plan (plans p.4):**
  Primary Bedroom, Primary Bath (with dual "His" / "Hers" areas + linen),
  Bath 2, Bedroom 2, Bedroom 3, walk-in configuration, and a labeled
  **`ELEV.` 4'-0" × 4'-0" elevator shaft** (framing only per scope) with
  a second 4'-0" element adjacent (likely the shaft's mechanical /
  overrun area).
- **Basement level per floor plan (plans p.4):** Storage, Garage,
  Lounge, Living, Kitchen/Dining, Covered Deck, Front Porch labels —
  these are labels on the *existing* structure showing what the addition
  ties into, not new construction (see Retrofit indicators).
- **Window schedule (plans p.4):** `01 3'0" × 1'0" TRANSOM × 1` and
  `02 3'0" × 3'0" D.H. × 1`. Plus TWO relocated existing windows called
  out on the floor plan and elevations.
- **Door schedule (plans p.4):** `A 32" × 1-3/8" panel × 2`,
  `B 30" × 1-3/8" panel × 1`, `C 28" × 1-3/8" panel × 1`,
  `D 36" × 1-3/4" exterior metal × 1`. Total = **5 doors** (4 interior
  + 1 exterior). Plus one relocated existing door.
- **Primary bath layout (plans p.4):** shower with bench + return
  (`RTRN. BENCH`), niche implied, dual-vanity zone with "His"/"Hers"
  split and linen storage between — matches scope's custom tile shower
  + 72" double vanity spec.

## Retrofit indicators (existing conditions the plans expose)

- **Two existing windows are being relocated**, not removed —
  called out on left elevation and back elevation (plans p.2) and on
  the floor plan (plans p.4: "RELOCATED EXISTING WINDOW"). Scope
  mentions window work but doesn't specifically enumerate relocations;
  this is labor the framing / exterior crew owns.
- **One existing door is being relocated** (plans p.4, "RELOCATED
  DOOR"). Same note as above — scope doesn't call this out explicitly.
- **Existing 6/12 roof slope** on the main house governs the tie-in
  geometry. Roof plan (plans p.5) explicitly instructs "CONTRACTOR TO
  VERIFY EXISTING ROOF SLOPES AND SOFFIT DEPTH BEFORE CONSTRUCTION" —
  discovery risk at the tie-in seam.
- **Existing chimney** shown on roof plan (plans p.5) near the tie-in
  area — must be flashed / worked around when new roof plane meets
  existing.
- **Existing plumbing, venting, septic, and HVAC locations are
  UNVERIFIED on drawings** — floor plan (plans p.4) explicitly says
  "*CONTRACTOR TO VERIFY LOCATION OF EXISTING PLUMBING, VENTING,
  SEPTIC TANK, AND HVAC BEFORE CONSTRUCTION." This is the drawing set
  telling the PM that concealed-conditions risk is real and unowned.
- **Existing floor plan labels** (Garage, Kitchen/Dining, Living,
  Lounge, Front Porch, Covered Deck) show the addition wraps a
  substantial existing house — the tie-in is not just a blank exterior
  wall, it's into occupied living space above/adjacent.
- **Bath 2 (`BTH. 2`) appears on the second-floor plan** — this is the
  existing hall bath receiving the acrylic-shower swap + window infill
  + repaint per scope. It sits adjacent to the addition footprint on
  the upper level.

## Things plans show scope doesn't mention

- **Two window relocations and one door relocation** — plans call them
  out explicitly (plans p.2, p.4); scope only says "install (4) new
  windows" + "install up to (1) new exterior door." The relocations are
  additional labor for framing (opening infill / new opening) and
  siding / brick patching that isn't itemized in scope.
- **Brick base on new addition** — elevations (plans p.2, p.3) show
  "NEW BRICK TO MATCH EXISTING" on the lower portion of all three
  visible sides. Scope's exterior finish section reads "Install vinyl
  siding to match existing as closely as possible" and does NOT
  mention brick. This is a substantial masonry line item (masonry
  crew, brick material, brick lead time) missing from the exterior
  scope.
- **A 7'-0" window head height** on one opening (plans p.2, back
  elevation) — most doors/windows are 6'-8"; this taller opening is a
  detail the framer needs to catch. Scope doesn't call it out.
- **Elevator shaft shown as 4'-0" × 4'-0" with adjacent 4'-0" element**
  on floor plan (plans p.4) — scope says "approximately 4' × 4'"
  framing only. Plans confirm the size but also show a second 4' zone
  next to the shaft; PM should verify whether this is the overrun /
  mechanical space or a separate closet.
- **Bath 2 layout and existing hall bath geometry** shown on the
  upper-floor plan (plans p.4). Scope treats the hall bath mod as a
  narrative bullet; the plan shows its actual location and adjacency to
  the addition, which affects sequencing (drywall tie-in, paint reach).
- **Ridge vent and drip edge** appear in scope but the roof plan
  (plans p.5) shows the actual ridge locations, valley count, and
  slope transitions — useful for the roofer's material takeoff (linear
  feet of ridge / valley / drip edge). Scope doesn't quantify.
- **Second-floor bedrooms 2 and 3** appear on the floor plan (plans
  p.4) as labeled rooms. Scope's OBJECTIVE only names "primary
  bedroom, master bathroom, walk-in closet" on the second floor.
  Whether Bedroom 2 and Bedroom 3 are *existing* rooms being redrawn
  for context, or *new* rooms in the addition, is ambiguous — see
  Discrepancies.
- **Dryer vent relocation** shown on floor plan (plans p.4: "RELOCATE
  DRYER VENT"). Scope mentions W/D relocation and roof venting
  generally; plan pins the dryer vent to a specific relocation task.

## Discrepancies (explicit scope-vs-drawings contradictions)

- **Siding material — brick vs vinyl.** Elevations (plans p.2, p.3)
  clearly show "NEW BRICK TO MATCH EXISTING" at the base of every
  elevation + "NEW LAP SIDING" above. Scope (Exterior Finishes)
  specifies "vinyl siding to match existing" with no brick. Either the
  existing house is brick-and-lap (the drawings say match existing on
  both) and the scope's "vinyl siding" is wrong / partial, or the
  drawings are wrong. **This is a change-order-sized ambiguity** —
  brick masonry is a distinct trade + material class. PM must resolve
  before demo.
- **Footprint sqft — 590 (plans) vs 789 (summary CSV).** Floor plan
  (plans p.4) totals `1ST FLOOR ADDITION 295 + BASEMENT ADDITION 295 =
  590 TOTAL ADDITION AREA`. The breakdown/summary CSV header says
  `Overall Footprint SF 789`. Possible reconciliation: 789 might
  include the existing bedroom remodel area (~12'5" × 16' ≈ 200 sqft)
  rolled into "overall footprint." PM should confirm which number
  drives the estimate.
- **Roof framing method — trusses vs rafters.** Scope says "truss or
  rafter framed, determined during design phase." Drawings do not
  specify either way; the roof plan (plans p.5) shows slopes and
  geometry only. **Unresolved at plan-set date.** Per company belief
  `stick_frame_default_for_small_additions` (590 sqft addition, roof
  span < 24 ft), default is stick framing unless PM confirms trusses.
- **Bedroom 2 / Bedroom 3 on floor plan.** Scope's objective lists
  only primary bedroom + master bath + walk-in on the second floor.
  Floor plan (plans p.4) shows `BDRM. 2` and `BEDRM. 3` on the upper
  level. Ambiguous — are these existing rooms shown for context, or
  new rooms in the addition? Signal C (spatial ambiguity) per retrofit
  heuristic — PM interview should resolve.
- **Elevator scope.** Scope says "elevator system not included, framing
  only + 30A rough-in." Plans show the shaft labeled `ELEV.` at 4'-0"
  and a second `4'-0"` zone adjacent (plans p.4). Whether that adjacent
  4' zone is part of the elevator shaft (overrun / equipment) or a
  separate closet is not annotated. PM to verify.
- **Window count — 4 (scope) vs schedule shows 2 new + 2 relocated
  (plans).** Scope says "install (4) windows including (3) 36" × 60"
  and (1) transom." Plans window schedule (plans p.4) shows only TWO
  new units in the schedule (`01` transom, `02` 3'×3' D.H.) plus TWO
  relocated existing windows. That leaves the three 36"×60" units in
  scope unaccounted for on the drawing schedule. Possible reasons: the
  schedule is incomplete on the extracted text, or scope over-counts
  the new windows. **Verify against original PDF drawing before
  ordering.**
- **Door count — 5 on plans vs 4 interior + 1 exterior in scope.**
  Scope says "(4) interior doors total" + "up to (1) new exterior
  door." Plans door schedule (plans p.4) lists 4 interior doors (A×2,
  B×1, C×1) + 1 exterior metal (D×1) = matches scope's 4+1. Plus one
  RELOCATED existing door not counted in either. Alignment is OK on
  counts; the relocated door is extra labor.
