# Tri-Cities Remodeling & Construction Co.

## Who We Are

Residential remodeling and construction contractor based in Johnson City, Tennessee. Core work: additions, bathroom remodels, kitchen remodels, sunrooms/four-season rooms, decks, and the site work that comes with them. Customer-facing design presentations (3D render packages) are produced in-house — designs are approved by client signature on the presentation set before construction documents are issued.

## Service Area

Home base is Johnson City. Core territory is the Tri-Cities: Johnson City, Kingsport, Bristol — Washington, Sullivan, and Carter counties. Jobs outside the core (e.g. Mountain City in Johnson County, ~45-60 minutes from Johnson City) are accepted but carry real drive-time cost: crew travel eats productive hours, deliveries and inspections take longer to arrange, and forgotten-material runs are expensive. Planning for out-of-core jobs should assume shorter effective on-site days and favor fewer, longer site visits per trade.

## Materials & Allowances

Client-selected finish materials (windows, exterior doors, flooring, fixtures, equipment like mini-splits) are handled as retail allowances in the proposal: the allowance sets the budget; client selections above allowance become change orders. National-average retail pricing is used at estimation time and must be confirmed against local supplier pricing before ordering.

## Change-Order Philosophy

Base scope excludes hidden and unknowable conditions: hidden rot, termite damage, inadequate existing framing, concealed utilities, rock excavation, groundwater, unsuitable soils, and code-mandated upgrades not visible at estimate time. All of these become change orders when encountered. Matching existing finishes (siding, roofing, masonry, interior) is best-effort — age, weathering, and discontinued products can prevent an exact match.

## How We Work

- Every job gets a scope proposal (client-facing, signed) and, where design is involved, a presentation set (signed) before construction documents.
- Field measurements govern over drawings; the contractor verifies field measurements, elevations, drainage, tie-in conditions, and utility locations before construction.
