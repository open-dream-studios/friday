# 308 Evergreens Scope - Key Details

## Project Address
**308 Evergreens**

## Project Overview
- Two-story addition: ~30' x 10'
- Basement-level storage space + second-floor expansion
- Primary bedroom, master bathroom, walk-in closet

## Code & Permits
- IRC 2018 and NEC requirements
- Contractor obtains permits and coordinates inspections
- Coordinate septic inspection with TDEC

## Foundation & Slab
- Continuous 12" x 12" concrete footings around perimeter
- 4" gravel base below slab
- Vapor barrier over gravel
- Reinforcement (rebar or wire mesh) in slab
- 4" concrete slab, smooth finish
- No CMU or full-height foundation wall

- Basement walls: 2x4 lumber @ 16" O.C.
- Floor system: dimensional lumber or engineered members
- Subfloor: glued and fastened

- (1) future elevator shaft ~4' x 4' (elevator not included)

## Roof

- Architectural asphalt shingle (match existing)
- Synthetic underlayment, drip edge, flashing, ridge vent
- Fascia board, fascia metal, vinyl soffit, seamless gutters
- Temporary dry-in measures included

## Exterior

- Vinyl siding to match existing (exact match not guaranteed)
- (4) windows: (3) 36" x 60" + (1) transom

## Plumbing
- Full mechanical, electrical, plumbing systems per drawings
