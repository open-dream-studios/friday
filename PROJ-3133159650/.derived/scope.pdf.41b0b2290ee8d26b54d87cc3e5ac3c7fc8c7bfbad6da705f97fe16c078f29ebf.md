# 308 Evergreens Scope - Project Details

## Property
- **Address:** 308 Evergreens

## Project Overview
- Two-story addition: ~30' x 10'
- Basement-level storage + second-floor expansion
- Includes: primary bedroom, master bathroom, walk-in closet

## Codes & Standards
- IRC 2018
- NEC requirements
- Local jurisdiction permits and inspections required

## Pre-Construction Tasks
- Site verification and coordination
- Septic inspection with TDEC
- Evaluate septic relocation vs. new tank/leach field vs. grinder pump to sewer
- **Note:** Septic/grinder costs NOT included, change order item

## Selective Demo & Site Work
- Saw cut/remove portions of existing concrete walkway
- Remove railroad ties and obstructions
- Excavate for footings/slab
- Relocate existing heat pump and electrical disconnect
- Extend gas water heater vent through new roof
- Debris removal, multiple dump runs

## Foundation & Slab
- Continuous 12" x 12" concrete footings around perimeter
- 4" gravel base below slab
- Vapor barrier
- Reinforcement (rebar or wire mesh)
- 4" concrete slab, smooth finish
- **No CMU or full-height foundation walls**

## Framing
- Basement walls: 2x4 @ 16" O.C.
- Floor system: dimensional lumber or engineered members per span/loading
- Subfloor: glued and fastened
- (1) engineered LVL beam (3-ply 14") spanning ~15'-6" for load-bearing wall opening, includes temp shoring
- All walls: 2x4 @ 16" O.C.
- Future elevator shaft framed: ~4' x 4' (elevator NOT included)

## Roof System
- New gable roof (truss or rafter framed)
- Pitch: 3/12 (ties into existing 6/12)
- Architectural asphalt shingles (match existing)
- Synthetic underlayment, drip edge, flashing, ridge vent
- Fascia board, fascia metal, vinyl soffit, seamless gutters
- Temporary dry-in measures
- **Roof tie-in assumes standard integration; additional structural mods NOT included**

## Exterior

- (4) windows: (3) 36" x 60", (1) transom
