**Project: 308 Evergreens Scope**

**Scope Overview**
- Two-story addition, approx. 30' x 10'
- Basement-level storage + second-floor expansion of primary bedroom, master bathroom, walk-in closet
- Full MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) included

**Codes / Compliance**
- IRC 2018 and NEC requirements
- Septic inspection required via TDEC
- GFCI/AFCI protection per code

**Change Order Triggers (not included in base scope)**
- Rock excavation / unsuitable soils / additional structural requirements
- Concealed wiring, plumbing, structural irregularities found during demo
- Septic relocation, replacement, or grinder pump system

**Demolition / Site Work**
- Saw cut and remove portions of existing concrete walkway
- Remove railroad ties and site obstructions
- Excavate for footing and slab
- Relocate existing heat pump and electrical disconnect
- Extend gas water heater vent through new roof

**Foundation**
- Continuous 12" x 12" concrete footings at addition perimeter
- 4" gravel base + vapor barrier + rebar or wire mesh
- 4" concrete slab, smooth finish
- No CMU or full-height foundation wall anticipated

**Framing**
- Basement walls: 2x4 @ 16" O.C.
- All exterior/interior walls: 2x4 @ 16" O.C.
- Floor system: dimensional or engineered lumber sized per span
- Subfloor glued and fastened
- (1) engineered LVL beam, 3-ply 14", spanning approx. 15'-6" to open existing load-bearing wall (includes temporary shoring)
- (1) future elevator shaft framed: approx. 4' x 4' (elevator system not included)

**Roof**
- New gable roof, truss or rafter (determined in design phase)
- Pitch approx. 3/12 tying into existing 6/12
- Architectural asphalt shingles to match existing
- Synthetic underlayment, drip edge, flashing, ridge vent
- Fascia board, fascia metal, vinyl soffit, seamless gutters

**Exterior Finishes**
- Sheathing + WRB
- Vinyl siding to match existing (exact match not guaranteed)
- (4) windows: (3) 36" x 60" + (1) transom window
- (1) new exterior door

**Electrical**
- (1) new 100-amp subpanel
- Full rough-in and trim-out
- (12) recessed lights @ $25/each budget
- (2) vanity light fixtures
- (1) vent fan/light combo in bathroom
- (1) outlet in upstairs storage; outlets in basement per IRC; (3) outlets in master closet
- Dimmer switches at bedroom and bathroom lighting
- Relocate (1) existing hallway switch (includes drywall patch/prime/paint)
- (1) dedicated 120V, 30A circuit for future elevator
