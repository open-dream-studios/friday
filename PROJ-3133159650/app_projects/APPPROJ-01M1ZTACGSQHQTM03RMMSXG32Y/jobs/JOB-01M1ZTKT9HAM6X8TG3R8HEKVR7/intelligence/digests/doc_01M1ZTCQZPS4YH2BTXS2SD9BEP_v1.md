## Digest

'308 Evergreens Scope' is a contractor scope-of-work/pricing proposal (no stated author, date, or revision) for a two-story addition (~30'x10' footprint) adding basement-level storage plus a second-floor primary bedroom, master bath, and walk-in closet, tying into an existing home. General conditions: IRC 2018/NEC compliance, contractor pulls permits/coordinates inspections, field verification of existing conditions required, and rock excavation/unsuitable soils/concealed conditions/additional structural needs are all excluded from base scope and pushed to change order. Pre-construction includes a septic feasibility study with TDEC (relocation, new tank/leach field, or grinder pump) but costs for any septic work are explicitly excluded/change-order — this is NOT a committed scope item.

Demo: partial concrete walkway saw-cut, railroad tie/obstruction removal, excavation for footing/slab, relocation of existing heat pump and electrical disconnect, extension of the existing gas water heater vent through the new roof.

Foundation: continuous 12"x12" footings, 4" gravel base, vapor barrier, rebar/mesh, 4" slab, smooth finish; explicitly NO CMU or full-height foundation wall.

Framing/structure: basement walls 2x4@16"OC; floor system engineered/dimensional lumber per span; glued/fastened subfloor; one 3-ply 14" LVL beam spanning ~15'-6" opening an existing load-bearing wall (temporary shoring required); all exterior/interior walls 2x4@16"OC; a future 4'x4' elevator shaft is framed only (elevator system itself excluded; rough-in is a dedicated 30A/120V circuit; coordination with "101 Mobility" if homeowner requests). Exterior: sheathing/WRB, vinyl siding to match existing (not guaranteed exact), 4 windows (three 36"x60" + one transom), one new exterior door, penetration sealing/flashing.

HVAC: one ductless mini-split, $2,500 budget, sizing pending load calc; relocation of existing HVAC components as needed.

Insulation: R13 exterior walls, R30 attic/roof, R30 floor between basement and living space; air-seal penetrations.
