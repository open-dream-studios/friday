(5) Wood-burning outdoor fireplace ~72" wide, height/configuration TBD by selected design + flue height + roof clearance + code; masonry or framed system with fire-rated materials, firebox, smoke chamber, flue liner, chimney to code termination height, spark arrestor, cap, flashing at roof/ceiling penetrations; footing/structural support per final design; fire-rated cement board/masonry backer substrate; stone/tile veneer customer-selected at $5/sqft retail allowance; hearth customer-selected at $5/sqft allowance; mantel (non-combustible, or wood only if clearances allow) at $1,000 allowance. EXPLICITLY REMOVED from scope: electrical circuit for electric fireplace and electric fireplace fixture allowance (implies an earlier revision) — so NO electrical, plumbing, or HVAC in scope despite boilerplate saying pricing includes them. Permitting, debris removal, equipment included in price. No start date, completion date, or duration stated anywhere. Existing conditions referenced: existing pavers, existing concrete fire pit, existing deck framing (posts bear into it), existing roof (tie-in), existing home wall (ledger). Fireplace subject to inspection-authority approval. This is unconditioned exterior space: no insulation, drywall (except fireplace cement board), interior finishes, windows, or MEP roughs. Customer selections still outstanding: fireplace design, veneer, hearth, mantel — canon forbids scheduling before final selections.
