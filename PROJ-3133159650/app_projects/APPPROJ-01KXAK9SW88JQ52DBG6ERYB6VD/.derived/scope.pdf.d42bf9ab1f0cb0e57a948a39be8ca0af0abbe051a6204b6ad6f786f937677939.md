**Project: 308 Evergreens — Scope Document**

**Project Overview**
- Two-story addition, approx. 30' x 10'
- Basement-level storage + 2nd-floor expansion of primary bedroom, master bathroom, walk-in closet
- Full MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) included

**Codes & Compliance**
- IRC 2018 and NEC compliance required
- Contractor to pull all permits and coordinate inspections
- GFCI/AFCI protection per code

**Septic / Site**
- Coordinate septic inspection with TDEC
- Evaluate septic relocation, new tank/leach field, or grinder pump connection to sewer
- Septic costs NOT included — change order if needed
- Saw cut/remove existing concrete walkway as required
- Remove designated railroad ties and site obstructions
- Relocate existing heat pump and electrical disconnect
- Extend existing gas water heater vent through new roof per code

**Foundation & Slab**
- Continuous 12" x 12" concrete footings at perimeter
- 4" gravel base + vapor barrier + rebar/wire mesh reinforcement
- 4" concrete slab, smooth finish
- No CMU or full-height foundation wall anticipated

**Framing**
- Basement walls: 2x4 @ 16" O.C.
- Exterior/interior walls: 2x4 @ 16" O.C.
- Floor: dimensional lumber or engineered members (sized per span)
- Subfloor: glued and fastened
- (1) engineered LVL beam, 3-ply 14", spanning approx. 15'-6" — opens existing load-bearing wall; includes temporary shoring
- (1) future elevator shaft framed, approx. 4' x 4' (elevator system not included)

**Roof**
- New gable roof, truss or rafter (TBD in design phase)
- Pitch: approx. 3/12 tying into existing 6/12
- Architectural asphalt shingles to match existing
- Synthetic underlayment, drip edge, flashing, ridge vent, fascia, vinyl soffit, seamless gutters

**Exterior**
- Sheathing and WRB
- Vinyl siding to match existing (exact match not guaranteed)
- (4) windows: (3) 36" x 60" + (1) transom
- (1) new exterior door

**Electrical**
- (1) new 100-amp subpanel
- ~(12) recessed lights — budget $25 each
- (2) vanity light fixtures
- (1) vent fan/light combo in bathroom
- (1) outlet in upstairs storage
- (3) outlets in master closet
- Dimmer switches at bedroom and bathroom
- (1) dedicated 120V, 30A circuit for future elevator
- Relocate (1) existing hallway switch (includes drywall patch, prime, paint)

**Change Order Triggers**
- Rock excavation or unsuitable soils
- Concealed conditions (wiring, plumbing, structural) found during demo
- Plan vs. field discrepancies
- Septic relocation/replacement
