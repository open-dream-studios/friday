# 308 Evergreens Scope - Key Project Details

## Property & Project
- **Address:** 308 Evergreens
- **Project Type:** Two-story addition, approximately 30' x 10'
- **Scope:** Basement-level storage + second-floor expansion (primary bedroom, master bathroom, walk-in closet)

## Codes & Standards
- IRC 2018 and NEC requirements
- Contractor obtains permits and coordinates inspections

## Pre-Construction
- Site verification required
- Septic inspection with TDEC
- Evaluate septic relocation or grinder pump to sewer line
- **Septic/grinder costs NOT included** (change order item)

## Demolition & Site Work
- Saw cut and remove portions of existing concrete walkway
- Remove railroad ties and site obstructions
- Relocate existing heat pump and electrical disconnect
- Extend gas water heater vent through new roof
- Multiple dump runs for debris removal

## Foundation & Slab
- Continuous 12" x 12" concrete footings around perimeter
- 4" gravel base below slab
- Vapor barrier over gravel
- Reinforcement (rebar or wire mesh)
- 4" concrete slab, smooth finish
- **No CMU or full-height foundation wall**

## Framing
- Basement walls: 2x4 lumber @ 16" O.C.
- Floor system: dimensional lumber or engineered members per span/loading
- Subfloor: glued and fastened
- **(1) engineered LVL beam (3-ply 14")** spanning approx. 15'-6" to open load-bearing wall (includes temporary shoring)
- All exterior/interior walls: 2x4 @ 16" O.C.
- **(1) future elevator shaft framing:** approx. 4' x 4' (elevator NOT included)

## Roof
- New gable roof (truss or rafter, TBD during design)
- Pitch: approx. 3/12 tying into existing 6/12
- Architectural asphalt shingle (match existing)
- Synthetic underlayment, drip edge, flashing, ridge vent
- Fascia board, fascia metal, vinyl soffit, seamless gutters
- Temporary dry-in measures included
- **Roof tie-in assumes standard integration** (additional structural mods NOT included)

## Exterior
- Sheathing and WRB
- Vinyl siding to match existing (exact match not guaranteed)
- **(4) windows:** (3) 36" x 60" + (1) transom window
- **(1) new exterior door**
- Seal all penetrations and integrate flashing

## Electrical (NEC)
- **(1) new 100-amp subpanel**
- Full rough-in and trim-out
- **~(12) recessed lights @ $25 each**
- (2) vanity light fixtures
- (1) vent fan/light combo (bathroom)
- (1) outlet upstairs storage
- Basement storage outlets per IRC
- (3) outlets in master closet
- Relocate (1) existing hallway switch (incl. drywall patch, prime, paint)
- Dimmer switches at bedroom and bathroom
- Standard outlets, switches, cover plates
- GFCI/AFCI protection per code
- **(1) dedicated 120V, 30A circuit for future elevator**
- **Assumes existing main service can support new subpanel**

## Exclusions / Change Order Items
- Rock excavation, unsuitable soils, additional structural requirements
- Concealed conditions (wiring, plumbing, structural irregularities)
- Septic relocation/replacement or grinder pump system
- Non-standard site access issues
