# Project Execution Plan — Lavinder Elevated Room Addition

## Snapshot
- **Customer:** Shane Lavinder
- **Address:** 333 Toy Crawford Rd, Kingsport, TN
- **Pre-construction start:** Mon, Feb 15 2027 (building permit application)
- **On-site start:** Mon, Mar 1 2027, 5:00 am
- **Job complete:** Wed, Apr 28, which is 43 working days on site (about 8.5 calendar weeks)
- **Scope:** Full demo and rebuild of a 430 sf elevated second-story room under the preserved shed roof. The room sits on 8 new hand-dug post footers with a 3-ply 2x12 beam and a PT floor. The work includes 63 LF of new walls, 6 stock 36x60 DH windows plus one 3068 exterior door, a vented ceiling, Level 3 drywall on walls and ceiling, a ductless mini-split, and conversion of the fused disconnect to a subpanel on the existing 200A service.
- **Key numbers:**
  - About 489 sized labor hours.
  - Four inspection events: footing, rough bundle, insulation, final.
  - Occupied home, with work done from scaffold on sloped grade.
  - No plumbing, paint, flooring or trim in scope.

### Insulation & Drywall

## Risk register

> ⚠ **Building permit not yet applied for.** File on Mon, Feb 15. If it is not issued by Fri, Feb 26, demo and every date after it slip.

> ⚠ **Mechanical Permit timing.** The schedule applies Tue, Mar 16 with issue due Mon, Mar 29, the day before Rough HVAC. File it with the building permit on Mon, Feb 15.

> ⚠ **Demo before materials.** Canon requires next-phase materials on site before demo, but foundation materials are scheduled for pickup Wed, Mar 3. Pull the pickup to Fri, Feb 26.

> ⚠ **Scaffold and protection come after demo in the schedule.** Demo lowers debris from scaffold, but site prep is scheduled after demo. Stand the scaffold and protection before the Mon, Mar 1 demo start.

> ⚠ **Siding crew double-booked.** Siding and soffit run the same days (Tue, Mar 30 – Thu, Apr 1) with the same 2-person crew. Add a second crew or let soffit trail; it has float.

> ⚠ **Ceiling vent path not settled.** The vented ceiling and baffles assume intake and exhaust exist, but roofing carries no vent hours. Decide before Tue, Mar 30 whether the 2 vent patches become vents.

> ⚠ **Subpanel conversion not yet confirmed.** It depends on the electrician's assessment of the fused disconnect. A replacement panel would add lead time and hours, and there is no load calc on file for the 200A service.

> ⚠ **Customer selections must be final before the job runs.** This covers the mini-split BTU (order Thu, Mar 18) and the lights and fans (order Thu, Apr 8). Get them in writing before Mon, Mar 1.

> ⚠ **Footing tasks are unvetted.** The Foundation Inspection and Foundation Pour rows are flagged unvetted. Confirm both with Will before Mon, Mar 8.

> ⚠ **Saw-cut count is assumed at 4 of 8 pockets.** That swings ±4.5 h. Rent a saw (it is not in the job breakdown), and write any rock hit as a change order.

> ⚠ **Plate height is unverified.** Wall, siding, insulation and drywall quantities assume about 8 ft. Field-measure before the framing package arrives Mon, Mar 15.

> ⚠ **Roof reseat assumptions.** The rafter count (16 in OC), tie-in length (63 LF) and roof pitch are unverified. Walk the roof framing during demo and adjust the roofing scope.

> ⚠ **Brick-wall finish not confirmed.** The brick wall gets surface raceway only. Confirm it stays exposed. If drywall is wanted there, it needs furring and a moisture plan, which is not in scope.

> ⚠ **WRB restored to siding.** Siding hours went from 40 h to 49 h for the 504 sf of WRB. Make sure the siding crew carries the WRB.

> ⚠ **Window lead time.** Verify the 2-week lead with the supplier when ordering Tue, Mar 9. A special-order unit would move install off Fri, Mar 26.

> ⚠ **No float in the schedule.** About 90% of tasks are critical, so any slip moves the Wed, Apr 28 completion. Look for safe overlap during the post-cure gap (Fri, Mar 12 – Fri, Mar 19) and the post-rough gap (Fri, Apr 2 – Fri, Apr 9).

> ⚠ **Scaffold strike not on the schedule.** It is billed on site prep but happens after Exterior Finishes complete on Mon, Apr 5. Schedule the crew explicitly.
