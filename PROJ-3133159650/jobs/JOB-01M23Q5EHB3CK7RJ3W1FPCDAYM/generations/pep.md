# Project Execution Plan — Sarah Lawson Screened Porch Addition with Fireplace

## Snapshot
- **Customer:** Sarah Lawson
- **Address:** Not on file. Confirm before permit submittal (Johnson City jurisdiction).
- **Scope:** A 267 sf single-story screened porch (22'-11" × 11'-8") on a new 4" slab with turned-down post footings. It has 8 posts, built-up beams, an 18-rafter shed roof tied into the existing eave, a vinyl-sided knee wall with beadboard on the inside, and screening plus a stock screen door. A manufactured wood-burning fireplace sits in a framed chase with a Class A chimney, finished with stone veneer, a raised hearth and a mantel.
- **Pre-construction start:** Mon, Feb 15 2027 (permit submittal)
- **On-site start:** Mon, Mar 1 2027
- **Job complete:** Fri, Apr 2 2027. That is 25 working days on site.
- **Key numbers:**
  - 4.2 cy ready-mix, delivered by direct truck with no pump
  - 4 cy gravel
  - 3.2 sq shingles
  - 107 sf knee-wall face
  - 146 sf screen
  - About 145 sf stone veneer
  - 24 sf hearth
  - About 263 sized crew-hours
- **Out of scope:**
  - No MEP work: no electrical, plumbing or HVAC.
  - No new windows or exterior doors.
  - No fascia, soffit or gutters on the new roof.
  - No yard restoration.
  - Field painting is unresolved.

> ⚠ **The permit and foundation inspection gate the whole job.** Demo cannot start Mon, Mar 1 without the permit in hand. The pour cannot happen Tue, Mar 9 without a passed foundation inspection on Mon, Mar 8. Either slip moves every downstream date day-for-day.

**The fireplace chain carries 3 days of float.** It runs:
1. Kit order
2. Chase install
3. Fireplace/chimney inspection
4. Veneer

If the fireplace/chimney inspection fails, that float is gone and the chain pushes into the walkthrough.

**Exterior windows & doors install is a zero-scope task sitting on the critical path.** Deleting it pulls siding and everything after it forward one day. That is your call (see Risk register).

### Rough Inspections — Wed, Mar 24

### Interior Finishes — Wed Mar 24 – Fri Mar 26

- Interior Installs are complete Fri, Mar 26.

### Closeout — Tue Mar 30 – Fri Apr 2
Run the customer walkthrough and punch list, the final building inspection, and one day of punch cleanup before the owner walkthrough.

> 👷 **Substantial completion walk:** Tue, Mar 30.
