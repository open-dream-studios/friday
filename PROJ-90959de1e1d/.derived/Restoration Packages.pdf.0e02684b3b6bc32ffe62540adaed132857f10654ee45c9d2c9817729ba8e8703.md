BASE RESTORATION
Essential mechanical and structural renewal — the foundation every other
package builds on.
$4,100
$5,325 without package · save $1,225
The Base Restoration Package returns the hot tub to dependable operating condition. It covers frame repair,
professional rebuilding of every pump in the spa, installation of a new ozone generator, a high-pressure internal
plumbing flush, a full insulation package, and comprehensive pressure testing of the plumbing system. The
plumbing system is tested and verified leak-free prior to delivery. The package also includes round-trip hot
tub transportation and a 90-day full-coverage restoration warranty.
What's included
n Structural & Frame: Replacing rotten frame boards with pressure-treated lumber coated in gloss black,
water-resistant paint.
n Electronics — Pumps: Rebuilding all pumps: replacement of both motor bearings, the ceramic seal where the
motor shaft enters the wet end, and all wet-end O-rings, then bench testing at 120V or 240V under 45 PSI of water
pressure to confirm leak-free operation.
n Electronics — Other: Installing a new ozone generator: removal of the existing unit if applicable, mounting and
securing the new generator, connecting the ozone air line and check valve, completing all electrical connections,
and testing operation and ozone delivery.
n Cleaning: High-pressure chemical flush through the plumbing system to break down buildup and dead skin, kill
mold, and clean the inside of the plumbing.
n Plumbing: Overage pressure test: all jet inserts removed, expansion plugs set in every jet hole, and 45 PSI run
through the plumbing to exaggerate any potential leak — the basis of the leak-free guarantee when the tub is
returned. Plumbing union gaskets replaced.
n Jets: Resealing up to 4 leaking jet bodies.
n Trim / Accessory Pieces: Replacing headrests (if replacements are available for purchase).
n Upgrades: Installing insulation package.
n Warranty: Full-coverage 90-day restoration warranty.
n Logistics: Round-trip transportation to and from the shop.

The Premium Restoration Package is a comprehensive mechanical, structural and cosmetic restoration. It
includes the entire Base restoration — frame repair, new ozone generator, high-pressure internal plumbing
flush, insulation package, full pressure testing with a leak-free guarantee, round-trip transportation and a 90-day
full-coverage warranty — with one change: the spa's pumps are replaced with new pumps rather than
rebuilt. On top of that foundation, Premium adds LED lighting, professional shell polishing, hardware
refinishing, shell-mounted component replacement, union gasket replacement, custom side paint and varnish, a
rodent protection package, a FROG water-care system, and the first month of spa care service.
Carried over from Base — included in full
n Structural & Frame: Replacing rotten frame boards with pressure-treated lumber coated in gloss black,
water-resistant paint.
n Electronics — Other: Installing a new ozone generator, including removal of the existing unit, mounting, air line
and check valve, electrical connections and testing.
n Cleaning: High-pressure chemical flush through the plumbing system.
n Plumbing: 45 PSI overage pressure test with all jets plugged — leak-free guarantee on return; replacing
plumbing union gaskets.
n Trim / Accessory Pieces: Replacing headrests (if available for purchase).
n Upgrades: Installing insulation package.
n Warranty & Logistics: Full-coverage 90-day warranty and round-trip transportation.
Upgraded at Premium
n Electronics — Pumps: Replacing the spa pumps with new pumps, in place of the Base package's pump
rebuild.
n Jets: Unlimited jet resealing — every leaking jet body resealed, however many there are, rather than up to 4.
Added at Premium

n Cleaning: Professionally polishing the spa shell.
n Refined Hardware & Fixtures: Refinishing suction drains, diverters, air control valves, waterfall control valves
and control valve components; replacing suction drain covers.
n Shell-Mounted Components: Replacing light lenses, waterfalls and LED cup holders.

n Upgrades: Installing the custom rodent protection package and replacing the spa sides.
