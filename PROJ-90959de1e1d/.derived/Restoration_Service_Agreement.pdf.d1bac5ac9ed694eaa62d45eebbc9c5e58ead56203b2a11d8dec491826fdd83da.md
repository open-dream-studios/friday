4. WHEN ADDITIONAL CHARGES APPLY
No additional charge applies for any service listed in Schedule A for the package selected. Additional charges apply only in the
situations below, and only with the Customer's approval in writing (text or email is sufficient) before the additional work is
performed:
Additional pumps — Premium only
The Premium package includes one new replacement pump. Each additional pump is $650. The Platinum package includes every
pump in the spa at no additional charge. The Essential package rebuilds all existing pumps rather than replacing them.
Damage found after teardown
Conditions that cannot be seen until the spa is disassembled — a cracked shell, failed electrical components, corroded plumbing
beyond the scope of the included work, or rot beyond the frame boards covered by the package. We will stop, document the
condition, quote the additional work, and proceed only once you approve.
Parts no longer manufactured
Some components for older spas are discontinued. Where a replacement is unavailable, that item is omitted from the work and its
value is credited against the Total Contract Price. Where an equivalent is available at higher cost, we will quote it before ordering.
Difficult access
The price includes a standard on-property move of up to two hours. Craning, fence removal, stair carries, or other non-standard
access is quoted separately before scheduling.

6. LEAK-FREE GUARANTEE
Before the spa is returned, the plumbing system is pressure-tested at 45 PSI — jet inserts removed and the openings plugged, so
the system is pressurised well beyond normal operating pressure. Any leak identified during that test is repaired before delivery.
We guarantee the plumbing system is leak-free at the time the spa is returned to you. If a leak in plumbing we serviced
appears during the warranty period, we will repair it at no charge under Section 7.

7. WARRANTY
Coverage. Full-coverage restoration warranty for the period shown for the package selected in Section 2 — 90 days for
Essential and Premium, 1 year for Platinum — beginning on the date the restored spa is returned. The warranty covers the
workmanship we performed and the parts we supplied. During the warranty period we will repair or replace covered items at no
charge for parts or labour.
The warranty does not cover:
• Damage caused by freezing, improper water chemistry, running the spa dry, or use of unapproved chemicals.
• Damage caused by misuse, accident, animals, weather events, or work performed by anyone other than us after the spa is
returned.
• Components we did not supply or service, and pre-existing cosmetic conditions of the shell or cabinet not included in the
package selected.
• Normal wear of consumable items such as filters, headrests, water-care cartridges and light bulbs.
• Any condition arising from the Customer declining recommended additional work quoted under Section 4.
Making a claim. Contact us as soon as the issue appears. We may inspect the spa before authorising a repair. Repairs under
warranty do not extend the original warranty period.
8. CHANGES AND CANCELLATION
Changes. Any change to the package selected or the work to be performed must be agreed in writing by both parties and will be
reflected in a revised invoice.
Cancellation by the Customer. You may cancel at no cost at any time before the spa is collected; the deposit is refunded in full.
After collection, you may cancel and the spa will be returned to you, and you will be charged for work already performed and parts
already ordered, plus transport. The balance of the deposit is refunded.
Cancellation by us. If on inspection we determine the spa cannot be restored to the standard described — for example a shell
crack that cannot be reliably repaired — we will notify you, return the spa at no charge for transport, and refund the deposit in full
less any parts already ordered specifically for your spa.
